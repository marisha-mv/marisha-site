The 10 Leaks

How many of these are in your funnel?

Check the ones you've already fixed. Your live score updates in the corner. Banding at the bottom.

Leak 01

Emails landing in spam

You're sending emails. But are they arriving? Most funnel owners have no idea their reminders, sequences, and offers are hitting spam. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC aren't configured. Your best copy goes unseen.

The cost: Every email in spam is a lead you paid for who never sees your offer.

Leak 02

Webinar show-up rates below 30%

Industry average is 12–15%. Benchmark for live should be 40%+. If you're not using SMS/WhatsApp backup, pre-webinar value content, and a 5–7 touch reminder sequence, you're paying for registrants who never show.

The cost: Doubling show-up rate doubles revenue from the same ad spend.

Example: Added a 90-second video to the confirmation page. Show-up went 26% → 47%.

Leak 03

No post-webinar follow-up sequence

The webinar ends. You send one replay email. Then silence. Meanwhile, 70% of your buyers need 3–7 days to decide. You're abandoning them right when they're most ready to buy.

The cost: $100K+ per webinar sitting in your "didn't buy yet" list.

Example: Added 48-hour replay sequence. Recovered $127K/month.

Leak 04

Underutilising your email list

You email when you launch. That's it. No weekly value emails. No upsell sequences. No cross-sells. No promotional campaigns. No dead-lead reactivation. Your list is an asset collecting dust.

The cost: A properly worked email list adds 20–40% to total revenue.

Example: A "breakup email" to 40,000 dead registrants recovered $147K (31 deals closed).

Leak 05

No upsells or order bumps

Someone's at checkout, credit card in hand, and you're only offering one thing. No order bump. No one-click upsell. No backend continuity. No high-ticket ascension path.

The cost: You're leaving 60–70% of customer lifetime value on the table.

Example: Added $12K continuity + $25K mastermind. LTV went from $3,100 → $11,400.

Leak 06

Selling features instead of transformation

Your copy lists what's included: "10 modules, 47 videos, lifetime access." Nobody cares. They care about who they become. Features inform. Transformation sells.

The cost: "A better course on Facebook ads" converts at 3%. "The 7-Figure Ad Account Blueprint" converts at 12%.

Example: Renamed offer from "Systematize" → "The Operator Blueprint." Close rate went 3.8% → 11.2%.

Leak 07

Slow page load speed

Your page takes 4+ seconds to load. 40% of visitors are gone before they see your headline. You're paying for traffic that bounces before the page even renders.

The cost: Every 1-second delay reduces conversions by 7%.

Leak 08

Price revealed too early

You show the price before stacking the value. Revealing $2K before building perceived value = 3% close rate. After a proper value stack = 12%.

The cost: $90K vs $360K revenue per 1,000 attendees.

Example: Moved price reveal from minute 40 → minute 68 with a $47K value stack. Close rate went 4.1% → 13.7%.

Leak 09

Fake urgency nobody believes

"Limited spots available" every single week. Countdown timers that reset. "Sale ends Friday" with no real reason. Your audience has been trained to ignore your deadlines.

The cost: Weak urgency loses 50% of sales to "I'll think about it."

Example: Changed "Sale ends Friday" → "Cohort starts Monday, 12 spots." Immediate purchases up 67%.

Leak 10

Single-channel dependency

You rely 100% on email. No SMS. No WhatsApp. No retargeting. No LinkedIn DMs. When deliverability dips or attention shifts, your entire funnel breaks.

The cost: Multi-channel funnels see 30–50% higher engagement and show-up rates.

Example: Added LinkedIn DMs to webinar invites. Show-up went 31% → 68%. Close rate 9% → 14%.
