# Masterclass Funnel Architect

Builds or audits a webinar/masterclass funnel the way a direct-response strategist
does: diagnose awareness and sophistication first, name a mechanism, then engineer
every surface from ad to upsell toward one belief and one sale.

## When to use
- You run (or want to run) a webinar/masterclass/challenge funnel.
- Registrations are fine but attendance or sales are weak.
- You're naming a masterclass or writing the class/offer and want it to convert.

## What I need from you
- Which stage you're working on (reg page, emails, the class script, the offer/close page, checkout, upsell) — or "the whole funnel"
- The format: pre-recorded with showtimes, or live
- The offer, price, and real guarantee
- Who it's for and what they've already tried
- Any funnel numbers you have (reg → attend → saw-offer → bought) and real testimonials (name + photo)

## How it works
**Diagnose before you write:**
1. **Market awareness** (how much runway before the ask): Unaware → Problem-aware → Solution-aware → Product-aware → Most-aware. A cold reg page, a post-pitch close page, and a last-call email each sit at a different level and are written differently.
2. **Market sophistication** (what you lead with): state the benefit → amplify it → introduce a mechanism → name/own the mechanism → sell identity. Saturated markets need a *named mechanism*, not a bigger promise.
3. **Name the mechanism** — specific, tangible, numbered and time-boxed ("4 techniques, 7 minutes a day"); acknowledge what they've tried and why it only half-worked; sell the shift, not the effort. Keep the in-program secret names *inside* the program.
4. **One belief through-line** — the single sentence that, if believed, makes buying the only logical step. Every surface proves that one belief with different evidence.

**Then build the stages:**
- **Registration page** — spoken feeling-question prehead → headline (named mechanism + outcome) → subhead (diagnosis + authority) → trust-badge bar → form in the first fold (copy left, form right). Score with the 4 U's: Useful, Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific.
- **Show-up emails** — pre-educate (give away one real lesson + an experiment), build anticipation; the final pre-class email is the sales carrier.
- **The class script arc** — Promise → Cost → Paradigm Shift → Teach + a live Experience → Gift-with-a-Gap + Yes-Ladder → Sale. Teach something usable today; create desire without handing over the whole method.
- **The close/offer page** — a close page for most-aware buyers: reinforce, don't re-sell; collapse to one decision; real guarantee; one real urgency element.
- **Checkout & upsells** — guest checkout; don't gate content; upsell pages are the highest-leverage copy — strip to one decision; price upgrades as the exact difference.
- **Post-class emails** — future-pace (what week 1 / week 4 / day 90 feel like); segment by behavior; last-call emails carry most of the revenue.

## Rules
- Pre-recorded is never "live" — scarcity comes from the viewing window.
- The title's mechanism must match what the class actually teaches.
- Every testimonial: real full name + real photo, always. Never invent a quote, stat, guarantee, or result.
- Judge a funnel on revenue per attendee / per lead, not vanity engagement.
- Measure show-up rate on every launch; read cohort performance over 30–90 days, not day 7.

## Output
Per stage: an audit (what's leaking → why → the fix, ranked by dollar impact) and/or
written copy (reg page, emails, script beats, offer page) with each change tied to the
metric it moves — plus scored name options when naming.
