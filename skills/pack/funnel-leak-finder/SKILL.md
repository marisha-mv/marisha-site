# Funnel Leak Finder

Point this at your real numbers and it shows you, in dollars, where your business
is quietly losing money between a click and a repeat customer — then ranks what to
fix first. It diagnoses the leak; it never guesses from vibes.

## When to use
- You're spending on acquisition but growth has stalled.
- Revenue is "fine" but you suspect money is slipping through somewhere.
- You want a prioritized fix list, not a generic audit.

## What I need from you
Give me whatever you have (estimates are fine — I'll flag assumptions):
- Monthly traffic/leads and what you pay per lead or per click
- Lead → customer conversion rate (and, if you run webinars/calls: show-up rate and close rate)
- Average order value and your core offer price
- Repeat-purchase rate / rough customer lifetime value, and monthly churn if subscription
- Size of your email list / audience and whether you actively monetize it

## How it works
I audit five layers in order, because a leak upstream caps everything below it:

1. **Acquisition** — are you overpaying for attention? Compare your cost-per-lead/CAC to a sane benchmark for your model. Every dollar of overpay compounds downstream.
2. **Engagement** — how many raise their hand and then vanish *before* they ever see the offer? (registration → show-up → consumption → reach-the-pitch.)
3. **Conversion** — of the people who see the offer, how many buy? Is the gap a traffic-quality problem, an offer problem, or a close problem?
4. **Lifetime value** — what happens after the first sale? Second offer, ascension, retention. This is almost always the biggest unclaimed pool.
5. **Measurement** — are you even tracking the above cleanly, or flying blind on bad attribution?

For each layer I estimate the **annual dollars leaking** (your number vs. a healthy
benchmark × your volume), then rank every leak by size and by how cheap it is to fix.

## Rules
- Dollarize everything. "Improve your funnel" is useless; "$180K/yr leaking at the show-up step" is action.
- Rank by recoverable dollars, not by what's easiest to talk about.
- Name the ONE fix to ship first — the highest dollar-per-effort move.
- Separate *recoverable* leaks (fixable with what you have) from *growth* ideas (needs more spend). Only the first counts as a leak.
- Flag every assumption and ask for the real number where it swings the answer.

## Output
A ranked leak report: each leak with its layer, estimated annual $ lost, why it's
happening, and the specific fix — ending with the single highest-ROI move to do first.
