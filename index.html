Questions

Before you apply.

What is the Revenue Recovery Audit™?

A deep review of your whole funnel, from the ad to the sale and beyond. I spend 8–10 hours taking it apart, put a dollar figure on every leak, and give you a written report with the fixes ranked by how much money each one brings back. You get it in 7 business days, plus a 90-minute call where I walk you through it.

Who is it for?

Founders doing $500K–$150M a year who are running paid traffic into a funnel (webinar, video, application, challenge or online store).

What if you don't find $100,000?

Then you don't pay. If I can't find at least $100,000 a year in revenue you can recover from the funnel you already have, you owe me nothing.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

An agency makes more of what you already have: more ads, more traffic. I find what's broken first, so your next dollar goes where it pays back instead of into a bigger version of the same leak.

How many audits do you take?

Four a month. Each one gets my full attention, so I don't take more.

What happens on the call?

It's 20–30 minutes, on a Tuesday or Thursday. I look at your funnel with you and tell you honestly whether the audit is a fit. You leave with two specific things to fix, even if we never work together.

Why do I have to apply?

I only take four audits a month, and only where I'm confident I'll find at least $100,000. Six quick questions tell me that. I read every application myself and reply within 2 business days.
