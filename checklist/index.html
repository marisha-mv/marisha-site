The same 10 leaks I check first in every Revenue Recovery Audit™

01

Get them to show up

Your emails are landing in spam

You're sending reminders, follow-ups and offers, but are they arriving? Most funnel owners have no idea. If the email settings that prove you're a real sender were never set up, your best emails go unseen.

What it costs you: Every email in spam is a lead you paid for who never sees your offer.

My email sending is set up and tested, and my emails land in the inbox

02

Get them to show up

Fewer than 30% of sign-ups show up to your webinar

Typical show-up is 12–15%. For a live webinar it should be 40% or more. Without text or WhatsApp reminders, something useful before the event and 5–7 reminders, you're paying for sign-ups who never come.

What it costs you: Doubling your show-up rate doubles the revenue from the same ad spend.

Example: A 90-second video on the confirmation page took show-up from 26% to 47%.

35% or more of my webinar sign-ups show up

03

Follow up

No follow-up after the webinar

The webinar ends, you send one replay email, then silence. But 70% of buyers need 3–7 days to decide. You're walking away right when they're most ready.

What it costs you: $100K+ per webinar sitting in your “didn't buy yet” list.

Example: A 48-hour replay sequence recovered $127K a month.

I send 5+ follow-up emails after my offer, each answering a real objection

04

Keep them longer

You barely use your email list

You email when you launch, and that's it. No weekly emails, no extra offers, no win-back emails for people who went quiet. Your list is an asset collecting dust.

What it costs you: A well-used email list adds 20–40% to total revenue.

Example: One “breakup” email to 40,000 cold sign-ups brought back $147K (31 sales).

I email my list at least once a week with a mix of value and offers

05

Get them to buy

Nothing else to buy at checkout

Someone is at checkout, card in hand, and you're only offering one thing. No add-on, no one-click upgrade, no next step for your best customers.

What it costs you: You're leaving 60–70% of what each customer could be worth on the table.

Example: Adding a $12K ongoing programme and a $25K mastermind took customer value from $3,100 to $11,400.

I have add-ons at checkout, an upgrade after purchase and a bigger next offer

06

Get them in

You sell what's inside, not who they'll become

Your copy lists what's included: “10 modules, 47 videos, lifetime access.” Nobody buys that. People buy who they'll become.

What it costs you: “A better course on Facebook ads” converts at 3%. “The 7-Figure Ad Account Blueprint” converts at 12%.

Example: Renaming an offer from “Systematize” to “The Operator Blueprint” took close rate from 3.8% to 11.2%.

My headline promises a specific result, not information

07

Get them in

Your pages are slow

If your page takes 4+ seconds to load, 40% of visitors leave before they see your headline. You're paying for clicks that never see the page.

What it costs you: Every extra second of loading cuts sales by about 7%.

My pages load in under 3 seconds (checked on Google PageSpeed Insights)

08

Get them to buy

You show the price too early

Show a $2K price before people understand the value and around 3% buy. Show it after, and it's closer to 12%.

What it costs you: $90K versus $360K in revenue per 1,000 attendees.

Example: Moving the price from minute 40 to minute 68 took close rate from 4.1% to 13.7%.

I show everything people get, and what it's worth, before I reveal the price

09

Get them to buy

Deadlines nobody believes

“Limited spots” every single week. Countdown timers that reset. “Sale ends Friday” with no real reason. Your audience has learned to ignore your deadlines.

What it costs you: Weak urgency loses about half your sales to “I'll think about it.”

Example: Changing “Sale ends Friday” to “Cohort starts Monday, 12 spots” lifted same-day sales by 67%.

My deadlines are real, specific and have a genuine reason behind them

10

Follow up

You rely on email alone

No text messages, no WhatsApp, no retargeting ads, no direct messages. When email has a bad week, your whole funnel breaks.

What it costs you: Funnels that reach people in more than one place see 30–50% better engagement and show-up.

Example: Adding LinkedIn messages to webinar invites took show-up from 31% to 68%.

I reach people in at least two ways (email plus text, WhatsApp or messages)
