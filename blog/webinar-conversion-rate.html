Webinar conversion rate benchmarks: the numbers I use.

These are the numbers I hold a webinar funnel against in an audit: cost per lead, show-up rate and close rate. Plus what a gap in each one costs you.

By Marisha Lakhiani·November 22, 2026·3 min read

“Conversion rate” for a webinar is really three numbers. If you only track one, you can't tell which step is costing you money.

The three numbers

NumberWhat I look forWhat I often find
Cost per lead (live webinar)About $15$50 or more
Show-up rate40% or more12–15% is typical
Attendees who buy12–15%Around 4%

These come from the funnels I audit. Your market and price will shift them, so treat them as a starting point.

What a gap costs: one real example

A coaching business came to me making $3M a year. Their numbers:

  • Cost per lead: $84
  • Show-up rate: 23%
  • Close rate: 4.2%

After six fixes and no extra ad spend, cost per lead was $47, show-up was 48% and close rate was 11.3%. That added about $2.5M a year. Results vary by business, but the pattern holds: three numbers, each a bit off, multiply into a very large hole.

Which number to fix first

If show-up is the weak one

Fix this first. You already paid for these people. Doubling show-up doubles revenue from the same ad spend. Start with the five show-up fixes.

If close rate is the weak one

Look at when you show the price and how you present the offer. Showing a $2K price before people understand the value gets around 3%. Showing it after gets closer to 12%.

If cost per lead is the weak one

Check that your sign-up page repeats the ad's promise, and refresh ads that have run for months. See the cost per lead benchmarks.

Don't forget the fourth number

Sales in the seven days after the webinar. A real follow-up usually adds 20–40% on top of what you sold live. If that number is zero, read the 7-day follow-up sequence.

Questions people ask

What is a good webinar conversion rate?

In the funnels I audit, I look for 12–15% of live attendees buying, with a show-up rate of 40% or more.

What is a good webinar show-up rate?

Typical show-up is 12–15%. A well-run live webinar should reach 40% or more.

How do I calculate webinar conversion rate?

Divide the number of buyers by the number of people who attended. Track sign-up, show-up and close rate separately so you can see which step is weak.
