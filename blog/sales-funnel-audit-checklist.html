Sales funnel audit checklist: 25 things to check.

This is the checklist I work from, grouped by the five places a funnel loses money. Go through it with your numbers open. Anything you can't answer is a leak worth looking at.

By Marisha Lakhiani·November 22, 2026·3 min read

Use this with your ad account, your email tool and your sales numbers open. Tick what is true. For anything you can't tick, write down what it might be costing you.

1. Getting people in

  • I know my cost per lead, and it is close to typical for my kind of funnel.
  • The first line of my landing page repeats the promise in the ad.
  • My pages load in under three seconds.
  • My ads have been refreshed in the last two months.
  • I am not paying to reach people who already bought.

2. Getting them to show up

  • My emails land in the inbox, and I have tested it. (How to check.)
  • My confirmation page gives people a reason to show up.
  • I send five to seven reminders, not one.
  • I remind people by text or WhatsApp as well as email.
  • 35% or more of sign-ups show up, or watch most of the video.

3. Getting them to buy

  • My headline promises a result, not a list of contents.
  • I show everything people get before I show the price.
  • I answer the five most common objections in the pitch.
  • My deadlines are real and have a reason behind them.
  • My checkout asks only for what I need, and offers an easy way to pay.

4. Following up

  • I send five or more emails after every offer.
  • Each follow-up email answers one objection.
  • People who miss the live event can watch a replay for a short time.
  • I email people who started checkout and stopped.
  • I write to my whole list at least once a week.

5. Keeping customers longer

  • I offer an add-on at checkout.
  • I offer buyers a next step after they buy.
  • I know what a customer is worth over a year.
  • A customer is worth at least three times what they cost me to win.
  • I ask happy customers to refer someone.

What to do with your answers

Count the gaps in each group. The group with the most gaps is where to start, with one rule: fix from the top down. More sign-ups won't help if they never show up.

Questions people ask

What is a sales funnel audit?

A step-by-step check of your funnel, from the ad to repeat sales, to find where you lose money and what each loss costs.

How do I audit my own sales funnel?

Write down your number at each step, compare it with a typical one, put a dollar value on each gap, and fix the biggest first.

How long does a funnel audit take?

A first pass with this checklist takes about 90 minutes. A full audit with dollar figures for every leak takes me 8–10 hours.
