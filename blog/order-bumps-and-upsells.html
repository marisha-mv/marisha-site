Order bumps and upsells: what to offer at checkout.

The easiest person to sell to is someone with their card already out. Most checkouts offer them one thing. Here is what to add, and where.

By Marisha Lakhiani·November 22, 2026·3 min read

You paid to get this buyer to your checkout. They have decided to trust you. For the next two minutes they are more likely to buy from you than at any other time. Most checkouts offer them one thing and say goodbye.

In the funnels I audit, that leaves 60–70% of what a customer could be worth on the table.

The three places to make another offer

01The order bump: on the checkout page

A small tick-box offer next to the pay button. It should cost a fraction of the main offer and need no explaining. Good bumps help people use what they just bought: templates, a quick-start guide, recordings.

02The upsell: straight after they pay

One page, one offer, added with one click. This is where a bigger offer goes: more help, more done for them, or faster results. Don't offer something that makes the first purchase feel incomplete.

03The next programme: in the weeks after

Your best customers want to keep going. Give them somewhere to go: an ongoing membership, a longer programme, a small group. In one audit, adding an ongoing programme and a mastermind took customer value from $3,100 to $11,400.

What makes a good extra offer

  • It helps with the same goal. Faster, easier or more complete.
  • It is one decision. One offer per step. A menu of choices kills the sale.
  • It is easy to say no to. A clear “no thanks” link keeps trust.
  • It is real value. If you wouldn't sell it on its own, don't add it here.

Don't break the main sale

Extra offers should never get in the way of the first payment. Keep the checkout short, and put anything large after the payment, not before. If people are leaving your checkout already, fix that first with my checkout guide.

In one audit, adding an ongoing programme and a mastermind took customer value from $3,100 to $11,400.

How to tell if it's working

Watch three things: how many buyers take the bump, how many take the upsell, and the average amount each buyer spends. Over time, compare what a customer is worth with what they cost you to win. I look for at least three to one. More in the 5 funnel metrics.

Questions people ask

What is an order bump?

A small extra offer on the checkout page that the buyer adds with one tick before paying.

What is the difference between an order bump and an upsell?

An order bump sits on the checkout page before payment. An upsell is offered on the next page, straight after payment, and is added with one click.

What should I offer as an upsell?

The thing that gets the buyer their result faster or with less effort: more help, more done for them, or the next level of the programme.
