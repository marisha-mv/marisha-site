VSL conversion rate: what's good, and how to raise it.

There is no single good VSL conversion rate. There is the rate your funnel needs to make money. Here is how to work yours out, and how to raise it.

By Marisha Lakhiani·November 22, 2026·3 min read

People ask me what a good VSL conversion rate is. My answer is a question: what does a visitor cost you, and what is a sale worth?

A funnel selling a $2,000 offer can make money at a rate that would sink a funnel selling a $200 one. So start with your own numbers.

Work out your break-even rate

Take what you pay for one visitor to the page. Divide it by what you keep from one sale. That is the share of visitors who must buy for you to break even.

$2 a visitor ÷ $400 kept per sale = 0.5%. Below that you lose money. Above it you make money.

Now you have a target that means something. In the funnels I audit, $25 is a typical cost per lead for a VSL funnel. If yours is far above that, fix the ad and the page before you touch the video. My cost per lead guide explains how.

Track four numbers, not one

One overall rate hides where the problem is. Split it into four steps.

StepWhat it tells you
Visitors who press playWhether the headline and page match the ad
Viewers who reach the offerWhether the video holds attention
Viewers who click to checkoutWhether the offer and price make sense
Clickers who payWhether the checkout is easy and trusted

The step with the biggest drop is the one to fix first.

How to raise each one

More people pressing play

Repeat the ad's promise in the headline. Remove menus and links. Make the page load in under three seconds.

More people reaching the offer

Open with the buyer's problem in their own words. Cut the long story about you. Check where viewers drop off in your video player, and rewrite that minute.

More people clicking

Show everything they get before the price. Sell the result, not the list of contents. In one funnel I audited, renaming the offer around the result took the close rate from 3.8% to 11.2%.

More people paying

Ask for fewer details. Add an easy way to pay. Email the people who started and stopped. More in my checkout guide.

Then add the follow-up

The cheapest sales in any funnel come from people who already saw the offer. A proper follow-up sequence usually brings back 20–40% more revenue on top of the first day. Start with the 7-day sequence.

Questions people ask

What is a good VSL conversion rate?

The one that makes you money at your price and your cost per lead. Work out your break-even rate first, then aim well above it.

What should I measure in a VSL funnel?

Four numbers: cost per visitor, how many watch to the offer, how many click to checkout, and how many of those pay.

Why is my VSL not converting?

Usually one of four reasons: the opening doesn't match the ad, the offer is unclear, the price is shown too early, or there is no follow-up.
