How to make money from your email list.

You paid for every subscriber on your list. If you only email when you launch, most of that money is sitting there doing nothing. Here are the five things to send.

By Marisha Lakhiani·November 22, 2026·3 min read

Here is a pattern I see all the time: a list of 50,000 people that only hears from the business during a launch. Every one of those subscribers cost money to get. Between launches they earn nothing.

In the funnels I audit, a well-used list adds 20–40% to total revenue. You don't need more subscribers for that. You need five kinds of email.

1. A welcome sequence

New subscribers are at their most interested on day one. Send three to five emails in the first week: who you are, your best idea, one piece of proof, and one clear offer. Most businesses send a single “thanks for signing up” and stop.

2. A weekly email

One useful email a week keeps people opening, which keeps you out of spam, which means your offers get seen. Teach one thing. Tell one story. Link to one thing they can do next.

3. A follow-up after every offer

This is the biggest one. Most buyers say no several times before they say yes, and about 70% need three to seven days to decide. If you send one replay email and go quiet, you walk away when they are closest to buying.

Use my 7-day follow-up sequence: one objection per email, with a real deadline at the end.

4. A next step for buyers

Someone who just bought trusts you more than anyone else on your list. Offer them the next thing: an add-on, an upgrade, a longer programme. In one audit, adding an ongoing programme and a mastermind took what each customer was worth from $3,100 to $11,400.

5. A win-back email

People who signed up months ago and never bought are not dead. Send a short, honest email asking if they still want help. One email like that to 40,000 cold sign-ups brought back $147K for a client.

Before any of this, check they arrive

None of these emails earn anything in the spam folder. Read why your emails go to spam first.

Where to start

  1. Write the follow-up sequence for your main offer.
  2. Start the weekly email.
  3. Send one win-back email to people who went quiet.

Questions people ask

How do I make money from my email list?

Email every week, follow up everyone who saw an offer and didn't buy, offer buyers a next step, and win back people who went quiet.

How often should I email my list?

At least once a week. Steady, useful emails keep you out of spam and keep buyers warm between offers.
