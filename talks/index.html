Part 6 The Framework
The biggest mistake: People get excited. Build random things. Zero business value.
4 Questions to Find What to Build
1
What do you do repeatedly?
Any task done 3+ times per week is a candidate.
2
What takes too long?
30+ minutes manually → AI does it in seconds.
3
What are you paying for?
Software, agencies, freelancers - can you build it?
4
What's blocking your team?
Bottlenecks, approvals, waiting - what could AI unblock?
Small team? Even better. Less coordination. Faster execution.
Agency? This is your edge. Deliver 10× more with the same team.

Part 10 Three Lessons
Lesson 01
Headcount is a tax.
Leverage is an asset.
Stop throwing people at problems. Start throwing AI at problems.
Lesson 02
Stop task thinking.
Start systems thinking.
Don't ask "How do I do this task?" Ask "What system can I build to eliminate this task forever?"
Lesson 03
Organize your data.
It's everyone's #1 job.
If your data is messy, your AI tools won't work. Organize before you build.
