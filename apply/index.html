Is this for you?

The method

Five layers. Eight numbers. One ranked fix list.

Most audits hand you 40 ideas. I measure the numbers that decide whether your funnel makes money, check each one against the benchmark for your funnel type, and put a dollar figure on every gap.

  1. 01

    Acquisition

    What each lead and each customer really costs you.

    • CPL
    • CAC
    • ROAS

    BenchmarkCPL $15 webinar · $25 VSL · $75 application

  2. 02

    Engagement

    How many of the people you paid for actually see your offer.

    • Show-up rate
    • Opt-in
    • Watch-through

    BenchmarkLive webinar show-up 50%

  3. 03

    Conversion

    Who buys, how much they spend, and what stops the rest.

    • Close rate
    • AOV
    • Checkout completion

    BenchmarkWebinar close on attendees 15%

  4. 04

    Recovery

    The buyers you already paid for who said “not yet”.

    • Follow-up recovery
    • Win-back
    • Cart recovery

    BenchmarkA 7 to 14 day sequence wins back 8 to 15%

  5. 05

    Lifetime value

    What a customer is worth once they've bought.

    • LTV
    • Retention
    • Upsell rate
    • Payback

    BenchmarkLTV:CAC of 3:1 or better

The number that decides it

LTV : CAC

What a customer is worth, divided by what it costs to win one. Every other number in the audit feeds this one.

  • 4:1+Strong. Room to scale.
  • 3:1Healthy.
  • under 2:1Scaling is losing you money.
  • under 1:1Every customer costs more than they're worth.

The layers feed each other, so the order matters. A $10 overspend per lead on 1,000 leads a month is $120,000 a year, before your funnel has made a single sale.

Questions

Before you apply.

What happens on the call?

It's 20–30 minutes, on a Tuesday or Thursday. I look at your funnel with you and tell you honestly whether the audit is a fit. You'll leave with two specific things to fix, even if we never work together.

How much does the audit cost?

We cover that on the call, once I've seen your funnel and know the audit is a fit. The guarantee is simple: if I don't find at least $100,000 a year you can recover, you don't pay.

How is this different from an agency?

Agencies improve what you already have, usually by sending more traffic. The audit finds what's actually broken first, so you know where your next dollar should go.

What counts as “recoverable revenue”?

Money you're losing right now to leaks you can fix in the funnel you already have. If weak reminder emails are costing you 200 attendees a month, that counts. “With more traffic you could earn more” doesn't.

How much of my time does it take?

About two hours in total: a short kickoff to give me access and context, and the 90-minute walk-through when the report is ready. I do all the analysis in between.

What if my funnel isn't a standard type?

I work across live webinars, recorded webinars, video sales letters, challenges, application funnels and online stores. The five layers I measure apply to any funnel you're paying to send traffic into.

What do I actually receive?

A written report covering every part of your funnel: what I found, how you compare to typical numbers, what to change, and the dollar value of each fix. Plus the 90-minute walk-through. Delivered within 7 business days of your kickoff.

What if I can't do everything in the report?

You don't need to. Every fix is ranked by how much money it brings back, and on the walk-through we pick the top two or three to do first.
