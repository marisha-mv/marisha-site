Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use marishalakhiani.com, how it's used, and your choices. It's written to be read, not to hide behind legalese.

What I collect

Only what you give me, plus basic analytics:

  • Your email address and name — when you request a free resource, subscribe to the newsletter, or submit a consulting/speaking/audit enquiry.
  • Details you type into a form — e.g. your business, revenue range, or what you need help with, when you submit an enquiry.
  • Basic usage data — pages visited and general location, via standard analytics cookies, to understand what's useful.

How I use it

  • To deliver the free resource or information you asked for.
  • To send you the newsletter and related marketing emails — only if you opted in.
  • To respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, discuss working together.
  • To improve the site and what I publish.

Who processes your data

Your information is handled by trusted third-party services used to run this site and its emails — including email/marketing platforms (such as Omnisend and Substack) and email delivery services. They process data on my behalf under their own privacy terms. I never sell your data.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time using the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Access, correct, or delete your data — just email me and I'll take care of it.
